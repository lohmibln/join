Privacy Policy

Overview

This privacy policy explains which personal data Join collects, why we need it and how it is protected. Join is a student group project of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH.

Responsible for the data

Responsible for the processing of personal data are the students listed in the Legal Notice. You can reach us at the email address given there.

Which data we process

When you use Join, we process the following data:

  • Account data: your name, email address and password when you sign up. The password is stored only in encrypted form by our authentication service.
  • Guest login: a temporary anonymous account without name or email address.
  • Contacts and tasks: names, email addresses and phone numbers of contacts and the content of tasks that you or other users enter. This data is visible to all users of Join.
