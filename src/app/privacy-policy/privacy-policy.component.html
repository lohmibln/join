Privacy Policy

Overview

This privacy policy explains which personal data Join collects, why we need it and how it is protected. Join is a student group project of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH.

Responsible for the data

Responsible for the processing of personal data are the students listed in the Legal Notice. You can reach us at the email address given there.

Which data we process

When you use Join, we process the following data:

  • Account data: your name, email address and password when you sign up. The password is stored only in encrypted form by our authentication service.
  • Guest login: a temporary anonymous account without name or email address.
  • Contacts and tasks: names, email addresses and phone numbers of contacts and the content of tasks that you or other users enter. This data is visible to all users of Join.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the data only to provide the functions of Join: logging in, managing contacts and organizing tasks on the board. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (use of the service you asked for) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our interest in a secure and working application). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Storage and hosting

Accounts, contacts and tasks are stored in a database of the service Supabase (Supabase Inc.), which also handles the login. The website itself runs on a web server of the Developer Akademie GmbH. When you open the page, the server may store technical data such as your IP address, the time of access and your browser type for a short time to keep the service secure.

Cookies and local storage

To keep you logged in, your browser stores a login token in its local storage. It is removed when you log out. Join uses no tracking cookies and no analytics tools, and the fonts are loaded from our own server.

How long we keep the data

We keep your data as long as your account exists or until you delete the contacts and tasks. If you want your account to be deleted, write us an email. As this is a student project, the data may be deleted when the project ends.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get information about the data we store about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • limit the processing of your data (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to the processing (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • file a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy when Join changes. The current version is always available on this page.
